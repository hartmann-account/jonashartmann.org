Building structures for businesses, families and capital.

I am an advisor, entrepreneur, investor and researcher in Zug.

For the past decade, I have worked across entrepreneurship, corporate advisory, technology and family governance — building companies, digital systems and organizational structures while developing an academic focus on finance, law and family enterprise.

Four areas

The four areas developed in parallel rather than in sequence, and they inform each other: advising is sharper for having built, investing is more disciplined for having advised, and research is grounded in all three.

  1. Advisory

    Advising organizations on transformation, transactions, risk and strategic challenges.

    Analyst, Risk Advisory at AlixPartners, Zurich, since 04/2026.

  2. Entrepreneurship

    Building companies, products, brands and digital infrastructure.

    Ventures since 2016, from managed IT services and web hosting to a recording label.

  3. Investing

    Investing across private and public markets, and in the governance of family capital.

    Chairman of the Board, Vereinigung der Familie Hartmann; advisory boards of Swiss Champions Fund and HWH Capital.

  4. Research

    Researching family enterprise, governance, finance and institutional structures.

    Doctoral research since 2026.
